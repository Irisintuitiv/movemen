The program
An 8-week transformational
group journey for men.

We meet once a week for 3 hours. The group is intentionally small — maximum 10 men — creating a powerful environment for growth, support, accountability, and transformation.

This is not a quick fix. We go deep. We work with the foundation of how you relate to your body, movement, expression, confidence, and presence.

If you show up fully and do the work,
you will leave this program a changed man.

The journey
Week by week.
Step by step.
Week 01
Grounding & The First Move
Goal setting, intention, presence, nervous system grounding, and beginning the journey.
Week 02
The 4 Elements
Exploring different movement qualities and energies within the body.
Week 03
Dynamics
Learning rhythm, flow, contrast, timing, and expression.
Week 04
Dance Foundations
Simple steps, grooves, coordination, and practical tools for social dancing.
Week 05
Freestyle & Flow
Freeform movement, improvisation, confidence, and learning to trust yourself.
Week 06
Leading Movement
Leading yourself, leading energy, leading a partner, and embodied direction.
Week 07
Find Your Style
Discovering your natural expression, personality, and movement identity.
Week 08
Integration & Expansion
Integration, reflection, celebration, and setting new goals for your continued growth.
